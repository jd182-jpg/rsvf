Stage 04 · Our story

Definitely optimistic.

Half a century ago, dreams transcended ceilings. Spaceships journeyed across the cosmos and mankind took a giant leap onto the moon. Infinite libraries of knowledge sat in tiny boxes on our laps. Visionaries knew paradise, and innovation knew no limits.

In recent decades there has been a shift. Instead of authentic, value-adding invention, we pivoted toward expanding and reconfiguring what already exists. Familiarity dominates. The future still holds bright promises, but humanity has lost sight of the path to reach it.

That did not discourage Jacob Straube and Pranai Reddy. In the summer of 2023, RSVF was born from two students and a podcast. Jacob hosted “The Data Room.” Pranai came on to talk venture capital. The idea that followed was a student fund that would give Rice students a real education in the asset class and give Rice founders their first capital.

They treated it like a startup: resourceful, resilient, adaptable. They pitched the Houston ecosystem, met founders, and learned how to stand up a fund from nothing. Rice’s undergraduate business culture funnels students toward consulting and banking. RSVF exists to be the hub that sends a few of them the other way.

Our advantage is the building we are already in. The Texas Medical Center is visible from the dorms. The Baker Institute is on campus. Lilie has topped the Princeton Review’s entrepreneurship list for five straight years. Most of all, the most capable minds of our generation eat in our dining halls and sit next to us in class.

We are not telling you we are coming. We are already here, we are hungry, and we are going to claw our way to the peak of venture capital. We hope to see you at the summit.
